Issue
This dispute asks whether Demolitions Co. should be allowed to end the case before it is ever tried, whether the facts are so settled that a jury has nothing left to decide. 

Rule
A jury is summned to find the truth where it is contested, where contest lacks, a jury is rendered inert. As a result, Rule 56 applies to govern summary judgment. Rule 56 holds that the court shall grant summary judgment if the movant shows that there is no genuine dispute as to any of the material fact and the movant is entitled to judgment as a matter of law. 

Analysis
In order for summary judgment to be granted, it must be shown that there is no disagreement on all of the following points, Pamela was injured or became seriously ill, the illness or injury was because of exposure to toxis substances in the workplace, and the illness or injury resulted in monetary damages to the plaintiff. There is no dispute as to whether Pamela fell ill as this is a fact that is evident by physical manifestation.
