- [`sateriale_case_brief.md`](sateriale_case_brief.md) — Full case brief of *Sateriale v. R.J. Reynolds Tobacco Co.*, 697 F.3d 777 (9th Cir. 2012), including the court's rejection of the "advertisement" characterization and its reasoning on the over-acceptance/limited-supply policy.

- [`dohrmann_v_swaney_brief.md`](dohrmann_v_swaney_brief.md) — Case brief of *Dohrmann v. Swaney*, 2014 IL App (1st) 131524: the outer limit of that rule — consideration so grossly inadequate it "shocks the conscience," compounded by unequal bargaining power, voids the contract.
- [`greiner_v_greiner_brief.md`](greiner_v_greiner_brief.md) — Case brief of *Greiner v. Greiner*, 131 Kan. 760 (1930): a family land promise enforced via promissory estoppel (Restatement § 90) after detrimental reliance.
- [`wright_v_newman_brief.md`](wright_v_newman_brief.md) — Case brief of *Wright v. Newman*, 266 Ga. 519 (1996): child-support obligation imposed on a non-biological father via promissory estoppel, including the Benham dissent on detrimental reliance.
- [`consideration_doctrine_synthesis.md`](consideration_doctrine_synthesis.md) — Written synthesis tying *Batsakis*/*Dohrmann* (adequacy of consideration) and *Greiner*/*Wright* (promissory estoppel) back to the Episode 6 trio (*Hamer*, *Dougherty*, *Kirksey*).
